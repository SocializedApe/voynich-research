# Voynich-like shapes in Bischoff's sign table (1954, pp. 24-25)

28 Oct 2026, from the MGH scan rendered at 300-600 dpi. Bischoff's table gives the letter values a-z of alphabets nos.
38-127 (plus sample lines of nos. 28, 36, 37, 57, 58, 59, 69, 74). Shapes compared by eye with the EVA glyphs; no
claim of a link, only of a shared shape vocabulary in dated secret alphabets.

## Section C: sign alphabets mixed with Arabic numerals (Bischoff nos. 101-108)

Zandbergen notes that several Voynich glyphs look like the numerals 2, 4, 8, 9 (and o, a, i, m, n like Latin
letters). Bischoff's section C is the medieval habit of exactly that: secret alphabets that use numeral shapes
among letter signs.

| No. | Manuscript | Date | Numeral-shaped signs (value) | Voynich-like |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 103 | Cambridge, Peterhouse 75, English marginalia | 14th c. | 8 (f), 9 (g), 6 (h), 2 (i), 3 (n), 0/o (e); also α (b) | o, 8, 9, α-shaped a |
| 104 | Prague, Metropolitan Chapter M. XVII, chemical recipes | 14th c. | 7, 6, 5, 4, 3 for l-p, 1 for r (key incomplete, inside the cover) | 4 |
| 105 | Paris, Arsenal 940, motto under the portrait of Jacopo Antonio Marcello (Venetian) | mid-15th c. | 0 (a), 2 (b), 4 (d), 6 (g), 8 (p), 7 (t) | **4**, o, 8 |
| 106 | Frankfurt, Barth. 91, subscriptions (1469) | 15th c. | 2 (e), 6 (g), 8 (s), 9 (p and q) | 8, 9 |
| 107 | Prague, Metropolitan Chapter M. CXXV f. 1 | 15th c. | 1-5 for vowels; 6 (d) | |
| 108 | Munich, Clm 14698 f. 307r | 15th c. (2nd half) | 1, 2, 3, 7, 8 | 8 |
| 106* | Wolfenbüttel 55.7 Aug. 4°, alchemical flyleaf | 15th c. | 1, 2, 3, 7, 8, 9 | 8, 9 |

(*listed by Bischoff under no. 106 as a second, alchemical example.)

So a cipher alphabet in which *4, o, 8, 9* stand as letters is attested in Venice (Marcello, mid-15th c.), Prague
(14th-15th c., incl. chemical recipes), Franconia and Bavaria in the Voynich century. Pelling's "4o" in North-Italian
diplomatic ciphers (1440-56) and this repository's florence1429 "4o" (code for Carlo, 1425) belong to the same pool.

## Other observations

- **No. 67, Fontana's *Secretum*** (c. 1430): built on circles and short strokes: o-with-tail (a), ∂-with-bar (b),
  small circle on a stem (e, o-like), o (i), a 9-like sign (g), ♀-like signs (o, p). The nearest dated "circle
  family" to Voynich's o / a / y / d group after Zolner's (`zolner_clm644.md`).
- **No. 72, Clm 641** (Hermann Schedel's volume): a "Chaldean numeral" alphabet, every sign a horizontal stroke with
  hooks (the style of Bischoff 149, numerals of the "Chaldean" system); not Voynich-like.
- **No. 74, Göttingen, Morbio 15** (Italian MS, a cipher entry dated 1484 among astronomical notes; Bischoff partly
  reads "(1484 ...) barbare(?) eid (= die?) 22(?)"): the pictured line has tall looped-and-crossed letters followed by
  e-like and ϱ-like signs, visually like a Voynich gallows + bench sequence. One line, a simple cipher, and 50 years
  after the vellum: a curiosity, recorded so nobody re-finds it as a lead.
- No alphabet in the table has Voynich's bench (ch/sh) as a unit, its gallows set as a closed group, or its
  i-minim series (in, iin, iiin) as variable-length units. The shared vocabulary is single shapes, not the system.
